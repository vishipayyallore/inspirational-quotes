## Xenial

**Meaning**: Relating to hospitality, especially being warm and friendly to guests or strangers.
**Usage**: "Cultivate a xenial spirit, welcoming new people and ideas into your life." - Encourages openness and kindness toward others and fresh perspectives.
**Grammar**: Adjective; chiefly formal; describes welcoming hospitality.
**Synonyms**: hospitable, welcoming, cordial, friendly, gracious
**Antonyms**: inhospitable, unwelcoming, hostile, unfriendly, cold

## Xenodochy

**Meaning**: The reception of strangers; hospitality shown to guests.
**Usage**: "Practice xenodochy by offering kindness to newcomers and making them feel at home." - Highlights generous hospitality and inclusive behavior.
**Grammar**: Noun; uncountable; a formal term for hospitality toward strangers.
**Synonyms**: hospitality, welcome, cordiality, generosity, guest-friendship
**Antonyms**: hostility, exclusion, inhospitableness, rejection, unfriendliness

## Xenophobia

**Meaning**: Dislike of or prejudice against people from other countries.
**Usage**: "Xenophobia can lead to division and conflict." - Fear or hatred of those from different backgrounds can harm societal cohesion.
**Grammar**: Noun; uncountable; refers to prejudice or fear directed at foreigners or unfamiliar groups.
**Synonyms**: prejudice, intolerance, bigotry, ethnocentrism, chauvinism
**Antonyms**: tolerance, openness, acceptance, inclusiveness, cosmopolitanism

## Xylophile

**Meaning**: A person who loves forests or woodlands.
**Usage**: "Stay a xylophile at heart, and let time in nature renew your focus and calm." - Encourages restorative connection with natural environments.
**Grammar**: Noun; countable; plural: xylophiles.
**Synonyms**: nature lover, forest lover, tree enthusiast, woodland admirer
**Antonyms**: urbanite, nature detractor, environmental indifferentist
